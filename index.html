My Projects

Dental Clinic OS - Clinic Management System

An offline-first desktop system that runs the entire daily workflow of a real dental clinic, replacing paper records. The reception PC acts as a local server and the doctor's laptop connects to it over the clinic network, so the queue, appointments and alerts update instantly on both devices through WebSocket. It covers patient registration and search, appointments with booking codes, a live queue with a waiting-room TV display, patient files with visits, printable prescriptions and attachments, billing with partial payments, financial reports and daily closing, expenses, inventory, automatic backups, and role-based access for the doctor and the receptionist. It also receives online bookings from the clinic's website.

Electron PostgreSQL + Prisma Real-time Sync (WebSocket) Live Queue & TV Display Role-Based Access

Dental Clinic Website & Online Booking

The official website and online booking system for the clinic. It is Arabic-first and fully responsive, with services, the doctor's profile, an FAQ and contact details. A booking wizard (service → date → time → details) shows live availability from Supabase, and double bookings are prevented at the database level. Every online booking syncs automatically to the clinic's desktop system and alerts the receptionist instantly.

Supabase (PostgreSQL) Row-Level Security Online Booking Wizard Double-Booking Prevention
